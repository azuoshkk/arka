  Technical Inquiries from IPQ Tecnologia to EngineAI




    To EngineAI Support Team: Below is the updated and simplified list of technical inquiries regarding the T800
    humanoid robot, organized by operational domains.



1. Maintenance & Servicing

   1.1. Routine Maintenance & IMU Calibration
   What are the recommended procedures for routine maintenance on the T800? Additionally, is it necessary to recalibrate
   the IMU after an impact, and how is that performed?



   1.2. Charging Base / Dock
   Is there a charging dock or similar base available to charge the T800 without removing the battery, while keeping the
   robot powered off?



2. Hardware Diagnostics & Troubleshooting

   2.1. Leg & Motor Telemetry
   Is there a built-in diagnostic tool, API, or specific procedure to inspect motor telemetry and health status (specifically to
   diagnose potential play/looseness in the left leg)?



   2.2. Component & Limb Replacement
   Can our specific model have its limbs replaced? If so, could you provide documentation outlining how to perform this?



3. System Formatting, Files & Software Architecture

   3.1. System Formatting, ISO & Specific Files
   What is the standard process to reflash or format the system? Do you provide a dedicated ISO/system image, and can
   you send specific system files upon request?



   3.2. NVIDIA CPU/Core Allocation
   How many CPU cores does the NVIDIA board use natively, and how many are available for our custom projects?



   3.3. Sensor Topics & Data Acquisition
   What is the correct method to enable ROS topics (or similar APIs) to capture data from sensors like cameras, IMU,
   LiDAR, etc.?
